TITLE: How Does AI Draw a Picture From Pure Static? #shorts
DESC: How does AI turn random static into a picture? Diffusion models like Stable Diffusion and Midjourney learn by adding noise to real images step by step, then training to predict that noise. To draw, they start from pure static and subtract the predicted noise a little at a time, steered by your words.
