TITLE: A Robot Hand That Walks on Its Fingertips #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Robot hand walking: ETH Zurich researchers turned an off-the-shelf robotic hand into a self-contained robot that crawls on its fingertips. It carries an 80-gram backpack (battery, motion sensor, Raspberry Pi Zero) and was trained in simulation with reinforcement learning, using "virtual springs" that pull each finger back to a home position. It crawled across 14 surfaces and righted itself in 21 of 25 trials.

Would you send a detachable hand into a place a robot arm can't reach?
