TITLE: Bigger AI Models Survive Faulty Chips Better: MIT Preprint #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Fault-tolerant AI: an MIT preprint (Oct 7, 2026) trained language models on simulated faulty hardware, where random blocks of the matrix math are dropped on every pass. The surprise: past a certain size, the models recover, as if they learn error-correcting codes like the brain's grid cells. It is a preprint tested up to about a billion weights, so whether it holds at frontier scale is still unknown. Could AI one day run on cheap, unreliable chips?

Sources:
Fault-tolerant foundation models (McCourt, Fiete, Chuang, MIT, arXiv preprint): https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10311
Grid cells generate an analog error-correcting code (Sreenivasan & Fiete, Nature Neuroscience 2011): https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2901
