TITLE: Gravitational Lenses: AI Found 70 Natural Telescopes #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
Gravitational lenses are galaxies so heavy they bend the light of galaxies behind them. A neural network scanned a sky map of nearly four billion objects, flagged 76 candidates, and the VLT's MUSE spectrograph confirmed 70 as real lenses. Would you trust AI to point the telescope?
