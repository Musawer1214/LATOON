TITLE: Brain-IT: AI Redraws What You See From a Brain Scan #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
Brain-IT is an AI from Michal Irani's lab at the Weizmann Institute of Science that reconstructs pictures people viewed in an fMRI scanner, using their brain activity. It predicts what is in the scene and where things sit, then a diffusion model paints the image. Presented at ICLR 2026. It works on pictures people saw, not dreams or private thoughts.

Would you want a machine that can decode your brain?
