TITLE:
Mistral Large 4: 1 Trillion Parameters, Mostly Asleep #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Mistral Large 4 has one trillion parameters, but only 49 billion are active at any moment. Mistral labels it a mixture-of-experts model, where a router decides which expert blocks process each token. Mistral launched the public preview on October 6, 2026, trained it on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in its own European data centres, and says the weights are due by the end of October. The benchmark figures shown are Mistral's own, and independent tests still have to confirm them. What would you run a trillion-parameter model for?
