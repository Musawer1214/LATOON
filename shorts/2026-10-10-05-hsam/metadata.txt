TITLE: Hyperthymesia: A Teen Whose Past Keeps Interrupting Him #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Hyperthymesia (HSAM): a 16-year-old known as KCL can recall what he did on most random dates, and his mind also drifts to his own past far more often than most people's. Researchers in Poland tested him remotely (Cortex, Sept 2026): on a random-dates quiz he scored 83% against a 28.75% comparison average, and 13 of 15 sampled off-task thoughts were autobiographical memories, versus about 39% in the comparison group. They suggest HSAM may be a lower retrieval threshold, not a bigger memory. It is a single case, so it needs replication. If your mind could replay any day at the faintest cue, would you want that?
