TITLE: Why Are Airplane Windows Round? The 1954 Comet Mystery
DESCRIPTION:
Every airplane window has rounded corners. In 1954 two Comet jetliners broke apart in mid-air, and the investigation traced the failure to fatigue cracks growing from window-area corners.

How the unseen works: stress concentrates around holes and sharp corners, and repeated loading grows tiny cracks (metal fatigue).

Notes: the force-line animation is an illustration (flow analogy), not a measurement. The 1954 water-tank test failed at a corner of the forward escape-hatch cutout; the Elba crash (G-ALYP) was traced to a fatigue crack at the roof ADF window cutouts. The Naples crash (G-ALYY) was judged possibly the same cause.
