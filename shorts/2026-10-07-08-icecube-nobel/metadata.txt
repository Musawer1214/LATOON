TITLE:
Nobel Prize in Physics 2026: A Telescope Made of Ice #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
The Nobel Prize in Physics 2026 went to Francis Halzen for IceCube, a neutrino telescope frozen into a cubic kilometre of Antarctic ice at the South Pole. Its 5,160 light sensors catch the faint blue flash when a "ghost particle" from deep space finally hits an atom, and because neutrinos carry no charge, their paths point straight back to their cosmic sources. If you could see the universe only in neutrinos, what would you want to look at first?
