# How Do Birds Sense the Earth's Magnetic Field? — visual concept (PLAYBOOK 5.0a/5.0b, written BEFORE building)

## What an ordinary viewer must SEE, even with the sound off
1. A small real bird flying alone over a dark landscape at dusk, with no landmarks: how does it know the way? (the mystery, frame 1)
2. Earth wrapped in invisible golden lines: lying flat at the equator, plunging steeply into the ground near the poles.
3. A REAL robin, with one golden field line passing through it and a plumb line for "down": the bird reads the ANGLE between them (not a north/south needle).
4. Push into the robin's real eye: the compass is somewhere in here.
5. Inside the eye: light strikes one protein; two partner molecules flash apart, tied by a thin thread; as the golden field line tilts, their glow changes. The field's angle changes how the reaction ends. (picture, not a graph)
6. Night on a campus: little wooden huts, buildings full of electronics; faint rust-coloured "static" fills the air and the bird's heading arrows scatter. Then a silver aluminium mesh wraps the hut, the static stops at the mesh, and the arrows line up.
7. A lab bench: a small glass vial of glowing robin protein between two copper coils; its glow flickers as the field changes.
8. Back to a real living robin; a dashed ring around its head: "not proven" inside a living bird.
9. A wide dusk sky over the land, a far flock migrating along faint golden lines: what else can animals sense?

## Look: "Migration Dusk"
The idea lives outdoors, at dusk and night, when robins migrate. So the world is a real-feeling dusk: warm umber-wine sky
fading to an ember horizon, layered land silhouettes, first stars. The invisible field is the one thing we draw: thin
golden lines (FIELD GOLD) that the viewer learns in the first shots and sees again in every scene (globe, robin, eye,
lab, sky). Real Wikimedia robin photos (credited) for the bird itself, graded into the dusk. Noise = rust static; shielding
= silver mesh; calm = aligned cream arrows.
Not the Rubin (deep space, tails) or Night Sky (forest->stars) look: this is landscape + real bird + macro + lab, separate
cinematic shots with motivated cuts (wide sky -> globe -> real bird -> eye push-in -> macro -> night campus -> lab ->
real bird -> wide sky). No graphs, charts or tables.

## Accuracy guards (DOSSIER + 2026-10-25 re-check)
- Inclination compass: reads the angle of field lines relative to gravity; does not sense polarity (Wiltschko 1972).
- Radical pair = "two molecules with linked electrons" (spin-correlated radicals); it is a model; label never says "proven".
- Oldenburg 2014: broadband electromagnetic noise (~50 kHz-5 MHz) from everyday electronics, NOT phones/health; screened, grounded aluminium huts restored orientation (Engels 2014).
- 2021: purified robin CRY4 in vitro (Xu 2021). 2025: pigeon inner-ear evidence (Nordmann, Science) keeps the mechanism open.
- Earth drawing: dipole approximation, illustrative.
