TITLE: Most Distant Fast Radio Burst: A Flash From 10 Billion Years Ago #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
Astronomers just traced the most distant fast radio burst ever detected, FRB 20240304B, to a tiny young galaxy. MeerKAT in South Africa caught the millisecond flash, then the James Webb Space Telescope found its host galaxy, whose stretched light dates it to about 10.6 billion years ago. The burst also works as a cosmic flashlight for the gas between galaxies. The study was published in Science on 8 October 2026 by a University of Sydney-led team.
