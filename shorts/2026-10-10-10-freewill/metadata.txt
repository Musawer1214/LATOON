TITLE: Do Neuroscientists Believe in Free Will? A 2,657-Person Survey #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Do neuroscientists believe in free will? A PNAS survey of 2,657 neuroscientists found that 64% agree every thought and feeling is reducible to the working brain, yet only 17.5% agreed that the nervous system leaves humans no freedom in their actions, and 59% disagreed. The authors suggest the answers may reflect cognitive dissonance, or two levels of explanation, a pattern close to the philosophical view called compatibilism. Only about 1 in 72 invited researchers replied and the sample skews Western, male and middle-aged, so it is a snapshot, not a verdict. Do you think a fully physical brain can still choose?

Sources:
Navarro-Peña et al., "Divergent philosophical commitments in neuroscience: Evidence from a global survey", PNAS 123(29), 2026: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2610776123
