TITLE: BC200: A Brain Gene That Still Jumps Through DNA #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
BC200 is a human brain gene that evolved from a jumping gene and, unlike almost all of its relatives, can still move through the genome. Cornell researchers found it inside a human poxvirus (Science, Sept 24, 2026), which suggests it once jumped from skin cells into the virus. What do you think evolution is keeping it for?

Source: Cornell University study in Science, "Escape of the BC200 gene to a human poxvirus reveals its persistent transposition in primates" (DOI 10.1126/science.aeh3441).
