TITLE: Why Is the Night Sky Dark? #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Why is the night sky dark? If space is full of stars, every line of sight should end on one and the whole sky should blaze. That puzzle is called Olbers' paradox, and its answer is mainly that the universe is not infinitely old: starlight has only had about 13.8 billion years to reach us. The poet Edgar Allan Poe guessed the idea in 1848. When you look up tonight, what do you think you're really seeing?
