TITLE: Diffie-Hellman Explained: A Secret in Public #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Diffie-Hellman is how two strangers can agree on a shared secret while everyone watches. Mix a public color with a private one, swap the mixes, add your private color again, and you both land on the same brown. Computers do the same with modular exponentiation, which is fast forward and brutal to reverse. Diffie and Hellman published it in 1976 and won the 2015 Turing Award.

Which everyday thing do you think is secretly doing this right now?
