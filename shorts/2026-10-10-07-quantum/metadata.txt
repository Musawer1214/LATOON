TITLE: Quantum Computer vs Chaos: A Tipping Point at 50/50 #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Quantum computer error control: researchers from Rutgers and IBM ran a tug of war on up to 100 qubits of IBM's 156-qubit Heron processor. One operation scrambled neighbouring qubits, like shuffling cards. The other checked qubits and reset them. Scramble more than half the time and chaos wins; reset more often and the system snaps into a controllable state. Right at about 50-50 the system flips abruptly, a phase transition, with nearly 5,000 two-qubit operations and nearly 5,000 checks and resets in the run (Nature Physics, Oct 9, 2026). Would you trust a computer that fixes itself mid-thought?
