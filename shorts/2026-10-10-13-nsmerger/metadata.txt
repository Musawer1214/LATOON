TITLE: Neutron Star Merger: The 10-Minute X-ray Flash Mystery #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
A neutron star merger flashed gamma rays for half a second on July 4, 2025, then X-rays kept pouring out for almost ten minutes, the longest prompt X-ray flash ever seen after such a merger. Astronomers think the collision may have left a newborn magnetar, but that is still a hypothesis from a single event. Mergers like this are also thought to forge heavy elements such as gold.

What do you think the long X-ray glow came from?
