TITLE:
Why Can't AI Just Say "I Don't Know"? #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Why does AI sometimes answer confidently and get it wrong? OpenAI researchers argue that a big reason is how we grade it: on accuracy-only tests, a lucky guess can score while "I don't know" always scores zero. On SimpleQA, a model that abstained on about half the questions was wrong 26% of the time; one that almost never abstained got slightly more right but was wrong 75% of the time. Their proposed fix: penalise confident errors more than uncertainty. Do you check the sources when an AI gives you a fact?
