# Sources and credits (Patriot clock Short, 2026-11-01 #1)
## Facts
- GAO/IMTEC-92-26 (4 Feb 1992), Patriot Missile Defense: Software Problem Led to System Failure at Dhahran: https://www.gao.gov/assets/imtec-92-26.pdf (verified in dossier research/2026-10-24-patriot-clock-rounding/DOSSIER.md via the globalsecurity mirror, now paywalled 2026-11-01). 25 Feb 1991 Scud hit barracks, 28 Americans killed; battery up 100+ h; time in tenths of a second, 24-bit registers; Appendix II 20 h = .0687 s / 137 m, 100 h = .3433 s / 687 m; fixed software reached Dhahran 26 Feb; rebooting resets the clock.
- Arnold, UMN, The Patriot Missile Failure (opened 2026-11-01): https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~arnold/disasters/patriot.html (1/10 chopped at 24 bits, error ~0.000000095 s per tick, x 3.6 million ticks = 0.34 s; Scud ~1,676 m/s -> more than half a kilometre).
