TITLE: Claustrum: The Brain's Most Hidden Hub, Recorded #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
The claustrum is a thin sheet of neurons buried deep in the brain, and on Oct 6, 2026 Yale researchers reported the first single-neuron recordings from it in humans. In seven epilepsy patients playing a spaceship game, claustrum neurons tracked uncertainty and prediction error, signals tied to learning and behaviour.
