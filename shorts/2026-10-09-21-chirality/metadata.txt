TITLE: Nobel Chemistry 2026: Why Life Is One-Handed #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
The 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry went to Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai for showing how chemistry can pick one mirror image of a molecule. Life uses only one hand of amino acids, and in a test tube chemists always got both. Kagan steered reactions toward one twin in 1986; Soai made a reaction in 2003 that formed only one mirror image, using autocatalysis.

Which mystery of life would you want chemistry to solve next?
