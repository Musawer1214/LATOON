TITLE: Hopfield Network Built From Atoms and Light: 7x More Memory #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
Hopfield network, rebuilt from atoms and light: Stanford physicists made an associative memory that recalls a whole pattern from a partial one, and stored up to seven times more than the classic Hopfield limit in a 16-spin network. The atoms even shift like brain synapses. Would you trust a memory machine made of physics?
