TITLE:
How Does ChatGPT Actually Read? Tokens Explained #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
How does ChatGPT actually read your words? It doesn't see letters at all. Your text is chopped into pieces called tokens, learned by repeatedly merging the letter pairs that appear together most often, and each token becomes a number the model works with. That's one reason letter-counting questions can trip it up, and why context limits are counted in tokens, not words.
