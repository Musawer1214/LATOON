TITLE: Your Brainstem Predicts What You'll Feel Before It Happens #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
The superior colliculus, a midbrain hub about 6 mm wide, was long seen as a reflex relay. A new 7-tesla fMRI study in Nature Neuroscience found its layers light up for the kind of sensation people expect, before it arrives. Part of the signal may be preparation for an unpleasant touch. Do you think your brain predicts more than it reacts?
