TITLE: Using AI for 10 Minutes Can Make You Give Up Sooner #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Using AI for 10 minutes can make you give up sooner. In randomized controlled trials with 1,222 online participants, people who solved fraction problems with ChatGPT open did better at first. When the AI was taken away, their accuracy dropped and they skipped more questions than the group that never had help. The effect also appeared on reading-comprehension questions. The authors suggest AI that scaffolds learning like a tutor. These were short online tasks, so the study shows an effect of brief AI use, not a lifelong one. Do you reach for the answer first, or the struggle?
