TITLE:
Can One Word Change What You Remember? #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Can one word in a question change what you remember? In 1974, Elizabeth Loftus and John Palmer showed students films of car crashes and asked how fast the cars were going. "Smashed" brought an average guess of 40.8 mph, "hit" 34.0. A week later, 16 of 50 people from the "smashed" group remembered broken glass that was never in the film (7 of 50 for "hit", 6 of 50 with no speed question). Have you ever remembered something that turned out wrong?
