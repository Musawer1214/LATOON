TITLE:
How Does AI Actually Learn? Gradient Descent #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
How does AI actually learn? By walking downhill in the dark. A model starts out random and wrong, and its wrongness is measured as one number called the loss. Picture the loss as a landscape: it can't see the map, only the slope under its feet (the gradient), so it steps downhill, measures again, and repeats billions of times. Step size matters: too big overshoots, too small never arrives.
