TITLE: Inside ChatGPT: Hidden Structure in Its Numbers? Yale Study #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Inside ChatGPT there are no words, only long lists of numbers. A new Yale-led preprint (Tom McCoy, Paul Soulos, Tal Linzen, Paul Smolensky) finds that the vectors inside large language models can be closely approximated by symbolic "role and filler" structure, like a pointillist painting that only makes sense from far away. It is a preprint, and the authors say it only scratches the surface. Do you think a chatbot "reasons"?
