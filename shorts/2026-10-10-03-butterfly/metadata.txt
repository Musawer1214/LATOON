TITLE: Why Are Butterflies So Hard to Catch? The Stripe Illusion #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
Why are butterflies so hard to catch? A new Nature study from the University of Exeter and the University of Essex finds that bold wing patterns and flapping flight create false motion cues. In high-speed footage, simulations of 397 European species, computer-evolved wing patterns and a touchscreen game with 100 volunteers, the patterns made the butterflies' speed and direction harder to judge.

Do you think stripes could work like this on other animals too?
