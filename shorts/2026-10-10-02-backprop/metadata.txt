TITLE: How Does AI Know Which Dial to Blame? Backpropagation #shorts
DESCRIPTION:
Backpropagation explained: how a neural network with a billion weights finds which ones caused a wrong answer. The error flows backward through the layers, the chain rule from calculus assigns each weight its share of the blame, and one backward sweep scores them all at once.
