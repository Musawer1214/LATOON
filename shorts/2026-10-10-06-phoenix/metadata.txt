TITLE: A Planet Born After Its Star Died? Hubble's Cold Case #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
A second-generation planet around a dead star? Astronomers re-examined Hubble data from 1999 on the white dwarf HS 0209+0832 (about 270 light-years away) and matched many unidentified lines to niobium, zinc and copper, elements heavier than iron, with almost no silicon or iron. That pattern points to a candidate gas giant that formed from material the dying red giant threw off. NASA's TESS also saw a signal repeating every 4.4 days, consistent with a Jupiter-sized world. It is still a candidate (Nature Astronomy, Oct 5, 2026). Could our own Sun's ashes do this one day?
