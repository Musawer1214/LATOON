TITLE: 3 Stars Born Together, 3 Different Fates: Pulsar Triple #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
Pulsar triple PSR J0435+3233: China's FAST telescope found a millisecond pulsar (one spin every 3.2 ms) orbiting a white dwarf every 8 days. Its odd, roughly 100x faster slowdown revealed a third, Sun-like star circling the pair every ~73.5 years. It is only the second known stellar pulsar triple, and the first where one star is still evolving. Chinese and European teams reached the same result independently.
