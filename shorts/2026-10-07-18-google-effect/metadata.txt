TITLE:
Google Effect: Your Brain Lets Go of Saved Things #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
The Google effect now has a brain scan. In a new fMRI study from University College London (Scientific Reports, 5 October 2026), volunteers saw faces and scenes and were cued to remember them, forget them, or "offload" them by saving them with a reminder. Offload cues produced brain activity that closely resembled forget cues, and the neural trace of the picture faded until it was statistically absent, while it persisted in the remember condition. The authors suggest this may be an adaptive way to free limited brain capacity for what matters.
