Can machines think? Alan Turing asked it in 1950, and 75 years later a chatbot was picked as the human in his own test. This film follows the question from Ada Lovelace and Turing, through Deep Blue, AlphaGo's Move 37, Searle's Chinese Room and a model that drew its own map of a game board, to the 2025 study and what is still unknown about machine minds. Would you have spotted the machine in five minutes?
