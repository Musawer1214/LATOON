Talking machines went from ELIZA in 1966 to ChatGPT and Claude today, and the question was never only how smart they got, but how much we trust them. In this animated archive, ELIZA, PARRY, ChatGPT and Claude walk through sixty years of their own record: a program that reflected words back, one built to be suspicious, assistants that agree too easily, and AI that now sits inside military targeting in Gaza and Iran. Every claim about the real world is attributed to its source. So who is still checking? Tell us in the comments.
