# DOSSIER: The Archive: Who Is Still Checking? (owner premium flagship, PLAYBOOK s25)

Status: pre-production, READY FOR OWNER REVIEW (not FINAL). Prepared 2026-10-15. Claim-level verification is in FACTCHECK.md; this file is the research brief and the source register used for the description.

## The spine
From a 1966 program that only reflected words back (ELIZA), to a 1972 program built to be suspicious (PARRY), to today's assistants that sound like they understand and agree too easily, and then into government offices and wars where software shapes what people see before they decide. The question is not "is AI evil" but **who is still checking**. The 1966 paper already named it: the responsible human must keep judging whether to believe the machine.

## What the research added to the owner's script (substance, not padding)
- **PARRY's 1972 test:** psychiatrists judged typed interviews; 21 right, 20 wrong. Experts were at chance, and PARRY says "remember that number" (R16).
- **The real ARPANET transcript (18 Sep 1972):** PARRY repeats "I went to the races"; DOCTOR says "Please go on"; it ends with a bill of $399.29 (R02). Comedy that is entirely true.
- **ELIZA's users could not type a question mark** (the MAC system read it as line delete) (R01).
- **Weizenbaum's secretary** asked him to leave the room so she could talk to ELIZA (R18).
- **1966, in his own paper:** "Important decisions increasingly tend to be made in response to computer output … A certain danger lurks there" (R01). This becomes the mid-video re-hook and the epilogue's open drawer.
- **ChatGPT's own launch post** warned it "sometimes writes plausible-sounding but incorrect or nonsensical answers" (R17).
- **Sycophancy research:** humans and preference models sometimes prefer convincing sycophantic answers over correct ones (R11); OpenAI's 2025 rollback (R12).
- **Claude's record, unsoftened:** WaPo reported Maven "powered by Claude" suggested hundreds of targets (R21), while an Anthropic-side source told NBC Claude sorts intelligence and gives no targeting advice (R19). The Pentagon has since replaced Claude (R20).
- **The Minab dispute, both sides on record:** Bloomberg's officials (R14) vs Palantir's CTO, "The Bloomberg reporting is wrong … the likely cause is human error" (R20). The Pentagon report is not public.

## Sensitivity rules carried from the owner master and s25
- No casualty imagery, no conflict footage that could pass for the incidents, no flags or weapons. Minab gets no music, keys or effects from S5-22 to S5-30, and a 2.5 s real pause after the casualty line.
- PARRY is never played as a joke about mental illness. His dry humour comes from the transcript, not from a diagnosis.
- ELIZA stays flat and literal. She is never wise or conscious.
- Claude is implicated, not a neutral referee: its own record is stated with both the accusing and the defending sources.
- Every contested 2026 claim is attributed in the spoken line itself.
