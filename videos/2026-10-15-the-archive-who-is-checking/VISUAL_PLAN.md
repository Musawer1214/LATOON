**Renderer upgrades for E (arc3d):**
- Candle key casts real soft shadows: a 6-face cube shadow map in a 3×2 R32F atlas (768² per face), 12-tap rotated-disc PCF; near plane 9 cm so the candle body does not shadow its own table. The light jitters with the flame, so every shadow in the room breathes.
- Emitters (flame, screens, sky pane) no longer cast shadows (moon and candle).
- `MAT_RAIN`: the window pane is an emissive night sky with slow cloud, static beads, running drops with trails and far rain streaks, animated by `time`. The moonlight carries a moving rain "cookie" (streak + mottle) so the shaft and the light pool on the floor ripple.
- SSAO-style contact shadows (screen-space cavity term from the depth buffer, half-res, blurred), applied to non-emissive surfaces.
- Dust motes are lit by what actually reaches them (moon shadow-map visibility + candle falloff), so they glow only in the shaft and near the flame, and drift as a pure function of time.
- Film grade (`film=1`): ACES → **display gamma (this was missing before: earlier renders wrote linear values, which is why they crushed to black and needed candy colours)** → split tone (blue-black shadows, warm highlights) → gentle S-curve → muted saturation 0.8 → lifted blue-black floor → vignette → luma-weighted grain. Halation: tight + three progressively wider bloom passes tinted warm (no ladder banding on the small flame). Up to 16 lights.

**Lighting design** (after Chris Brejon, *CG Cinematography* ch. 8 "Shot Lighting" — practicals first, then key/wrap/rim per character, complementary colour directions, volumetrics for depth, "a bright object between two characters ties them": https://chrisbrejon.com/cg-cinematography/chapter-8-shot-lighting/ ; and Blender Studio's key / key-soft / fill / rim rig: https://studio.blender.org/tools/artist-guide/shot-production/lighting): the candle is the only key and the brightest object, placed between ChatGPT and Claude; cool moonlight enters through a tall arched window back-left (shaft, rim, floor pool); per character a small warm wrap from the candle side and a cool rim from behind so each silhouette separates from the dark shelves; soft cool fills on the back shelves so the room reads; a warm table-bounce onto the floor; each screen face casts a faint light of its colour.

**What arc3d does per frame (all GLSL):** sun shadow map (2048², 3×3 PCF) → lit pass (procedural wood grain and plank seams, leather spines with gilt bands, stone, brass specular) with up to 12 practical lights. **The avatars are real point lights**, so they light the shelves, table and floor around them → ray-marched volumetric in-scatter through the shadow map (god rays and lamp haze, 72 steps, half-res, blurred) → depth-tested dust motes → depth of field (CoC gather, 40 taps, per-sample CoC weighting) → soft bloom → ACES tone map + saturation (vivid, never muddy) → **world-space steel-engraving hatching in the shadows** (45° plus cross-hatch in the deepest tones, fading where lines would go sub-pixel) → film grain + vignette.

**Render budget (sandbox):** ~10:58 at 30 fps ≈ 19,700 frames × ~3.6 s ≈ **~20 h** of background rendering, in 45–90-frame chunks (background jobs work here). Plan to cut this roughly in half: hold shots during dialogue reuse a rendered plate with code-driven 2.5D parallax/push from the depth buffer (arc3d writes depth), rendering full 3D only for true camera moves. Estimate **~8–10 h**. The same code runs on the owner's PC (`pip install moderngl numpy pillow`; standalone context on the Radeon 780M), probably 10–30× faster (untested).

**Rig (characters.py, reusable for every shot):** parts root > hips > body > head (+ face), arms, legs, each with a pivot. `Character.pose(t, CharState)` returns world-space triangles for `Renderer.set_dynamic`. Drives: position/yaw, walk speed + phase (from `Track`: phase is derived from distance travelled so feet plant instead of skating), talk amplitude, emotion, look target (head yaw/pitch clamped so faces cheat toward camera), gesture weight, typed-character count. Walk cycle: leg swing sized from step length, foot lift on the forward pass, hips lowest at contact and highest at passing, squash on contact / stretch on passing, head overlap lag, weight shift over the stance leg, lean, opposite arm swing. Idle: breathing scale, sway, personality extras. Blinks: personality interval with irregular timing and occasional double blinks. **Lip-sync:** `envelope(wav)` gives per-frame loudness (RMS relative to the clip's peaks + syllable contrast + attack/release), which opens each mouth in its own style and adds talk nods. Phoneme timing can replace the envelope later (same 0..1 input).
