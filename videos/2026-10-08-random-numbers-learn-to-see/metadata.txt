DESCRIPTION:
How do neural networks learn? A neural network starts as a pile of random numbers and ends up recognizing a cat. In this long-form LATOON explainer we follow one small network from its first wrong guess to its first right answer: pixels as numbers, a single neuron (weighted sum + ReLU), layers, the loss, the loss landscape, gradient descent and the learning rate, backpropagation and the chain rule, mini-batches and SGD, what the layers actually learn, and the honest open questions researchers are still working on.

Made for curious beginners, with the real math shown lightly for engineers and researchers. All animation and music are original and code-made.

Which part surprised you most: the chain rule, or that networks can memorize random labels? Tell us in the comments.
